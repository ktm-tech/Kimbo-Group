About Kimbo Group

Built on quality. Driven by results.

Kimbo Group is a South African construction and civil engineering company based in Gauteng, South Africa, serving residential, commercial and property development projects.

The company combines construction expertise, technical design, project coordination and hands-on implementation to provide practical solutions from concept through to completion — with a focus on quality workmanship, effective project management and professional service. The company is led by Managing Director Thabo Mpofu.
